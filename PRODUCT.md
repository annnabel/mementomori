# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

One person, alone, usually in the evening. They open the page deliberately — to see, honestly, how much of their life has passed and how much may remain. No workflow, no account, no feed. A single contemplative sitting.

## Product Purpose

A memento mori: your life rendered as a grid of weeks (one row per year, one square per week, to a chosen horizon such as 90 years). Enter a date of birth; the weeks already lived light up, the current week burns, the rest wait. Success is a quiet jolt — the visitor closes the tab resolved to spend the week well.

## Brand Personality

Still, vast, finite. The tone of a twilight sky over water: calm and enormous, never gothic or morbid. Sober prose, no exclamation marks, no gamification.

## Anti-references

- Productivity-app cheerfulness (streaks, confetti, "you got this!").
- Gothic/horror styling — skulls, blackletter, blood red.
- SaaS dashboard grammar: stat tiles, KPI cards, gradient CTAs.

## Design Principles

- **The grid is the message.** Everything else (copy, controls, chrome) stays subordinate to the field of weeks.
- **One flame.** The current week is the only warm element on the page; scarcity gives it weight.
- **Numbers as prose.** Facts are delivered in sentences, not stat tiles — this is an inscription, not a dashboard.
- **Quiet motion.** One deliberate fill sweep on load; nothing else moves without cause.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum: body text ≥ 4.5:1 on the dark surface, full keyboard operability for the two inputs, `prefers-reduced-motion` honored (instant fill, static glow), grid exposed to assistive tech as a labelled image with the same facts available as text.
