## Anti-Patterns Verdict

**LLM assessment: PASS — this does not read as AI-made.** Committed dark ground (no cream cliché), no gradient text, no side-stripes, no card grids, no hero-metric tiles, no numbered scaffolding, zero horizontal overflow at 390px and 1440px (measured). The Newsreader optical serif, single semantic amber accent, and editorial copy voice read as authored. Surviving tics: two uppercase tracked micro-labels in the hero form, em-dash-heavy microcopy — sparing and systematic, acceptable.

**Deterministic scan:** CLI scan of `index.html` and `app.js`: **0 findings** (exit 0, both clean). In-browser detector (post-activation): **3 findings** —
1. `line-length` — `.source` ("U.S. data — American Time Use Survey…", index.html:375) runs ~97ch/line on desktop (target <80). Real; fix with a max-width.
2. `tight-leading` — `.reframe blockquote` line-height 1.26 (index.html:263) vs ≥1.3 threshold. Borderline: DESIGN.md commits 1.26 for the display quote, but light-on-dark type wants extra leading; a nudge to ~1.3 costs nothing.
3. `all-caps-body` — `.attr` "SENECA — ON THE SHORTNESS OF LIFE" (33 chars, index.html:272). **False positive in spirit**: DESIGN.md explicitly reserves uppercase for "short labels and the quote attribution"; this is the committed brand system, not body copy. Candidate for `.impeccable/critique/ignore.md`.

The detector caught the `.source` line length that the design review missed; the design review caught canvas-level bugs no static detector can see.

**Visual overlays:** the environment is headless — no user-visible browser tab or overlay could be presented. Fallback signal: detector findings were captured via headless console injection (script mutation preflight succeeded; detector ran in-page twice, pre- and post-activation).

## Overall Impression

This is polished, intentional work — a committed identity (amber = now; ivory = you), unusually deep canvas accessibility, and an emotional arc that actually lands, ending on agency as specced. The single biggest opportunity: **the product is invisible at the moment of maximum skepticism.** The hero asks a stranger for their birthdate before showing a single dot. Second: two precision bugs (the bonus-time ember, the "YOU ARE HERE" mislabel) undercut the brand's core claim that precision earns the emotion.

## What's Working

1. **Committed, coherent identity.** One accent with a semantic rule, real typographic POV, canvas surfaces (grid, chart, share card) that match the DOM tokens exactly. Nothing is decoration.
2. **Accessibility depth rare for canvas-heavy pages**: `aria-hidden` grid with live text-equivalent caption, chart as keyboard-operable `role="slider"` with full `aria-valuetext`, DOM readout instead of canvas tooltips, reduced-motion honored in CSS and JS.
3. **Trust engineering at activation**: privacy line directly under the date field, exact numbers, visible caveats, `?age=` never carries birthdates.

## Priority Issues

**[P1] The core artifact is invisible before activation** — the hero is text + a form; the 8-second skeptical visitor never glimpses the dot grid they're asked to unlock, yet ≥70% activation is the success metric. **Fix:** render a faint ghost grid (all hollow dots, one pulsing ember) behind/beneath the hero form — zero data needed, pure preview. **Suggested command:** /impeccable onboard

**[P1] Bonus-time state kills the ember** — for age ≥ expectancy, `startGrid` sets `current = -1` and `makeCard()`'s `i === capped` never fires: the grid renders 100% filled with no amber dot, and the share card matches. The one persona PRODUCT.md explicitly protects gets a "life complete" visual at the exact moment the "still burning" symbol matters most. **Fix:** clamp the ember to the last cell in both `startGrid` (app.js:126) and `makeCard` (app.js:358). **Suggested command:** /impeccable harden

**[P2] "YOU ARE HERE · 60" lies when scrubbed** — `drawChart()` (app.js:238) labels the scrub line "YOU ARE HERE" at whatever age is scrubbed, and permanently shows 80 for users over 80. Violates design principle #3 (precision earns the emotion). **Fix:** render "YOU ARE HERE" only when scrub equals the user's (clamped) age; otherwise label "AGE N", keeping an amber tick at the true age. **Suggested command:** /impeccable harden

**[P2] Life-expectancy basis is an up-front toll** — a three-button segmented control plus duplicate microcopy sits between the trust moment and the CTA; "Average" is already the default and the toggle already re-renders post-submit. Extra decision + jargon at the highest-dropout moment. **Fix:** drop it from the hero; expose "Assumes 83.1 years — change" inline in the calendar small-print. **Suggested command:** /impeccable distill

**[P2] No edit path after submit** — a mistyped year means scrolling ~3,000px back to the form with no signpost. **Fix:** quiet text link near the small-print ("Born 14 May 1993 — edit") that scrolls to and focuses `#dob`. **Suggested command:** /impeccable harden

**[P3] Small frictions** — readout re-sorts by value each scrub (tracked series hops position; keep `SERIES_ORDER`); desktop share fallback copies AND force-downloads simultaneously; `.source` ~97ch line; quote leading 1.26 → ~1.3. **Suggested command:** /impeccable polish

## Persona Red Flags

**Casey (distracted mobile, one-handed, 4G):** Performance is excellent (zero deps, ~101KB subsetted fonts, no images). But "Female · 85.1 y" / "Male · 81.1 y" are cryptic at a glance and cost seconds Casey doesn't have; and post-submit auto-scroll lands atop a ~1,080px grid on an 844px screen — the payoff caption ("You have lived 1,732…") is below the fold mid-animation and can be missed entirely.

**Jordan (zero context, from an Instagram story):** Plain-URL arrival gives no glimpse of what "See yours" produces — the birthdate ask precedes any visible product (largest activation risk). "Life expectancy at birth in Australia" is an unexplained head-scratcher for a global viewer; one clause of justification would remove the blink. `?age=31` arrival works cleanly and never leaks sharer data. (Note: `og:url` pointing at `annnabel.github.io` is correct — the repo owner is `annnabel`; not a bug.)

**Sam (screen reader / keyboard only):** Strong foundation, three gaps: (1) after submit, focus stays on the submit button while four sections silently append — move focus to `#cap-main` (`tabindex="-1"`); (2) the only chart instruction ("Drag… Tap…") is pointer-only; sighted keyboard users get no hint arrows work; (3) `.seg` and `.legend` buttons use default UA focus rings instead of the amber `focus-visible` treatment given to fields and the canvas — inconsistent focus language.

## Minor Observations

- Amber doubles as the error color — sanctioned by DESIGN.md, but it semantically collides with "amber = the present moment."
- `insightFor()` tells every 36–55-year-old "time with your children is peaking now," childless or not; the aggregate framing barely covers it.
- "That date didn't parse — try again" and "This works for ages up to 99" should match the register of the best error ("Enter your birthdate to begin.").
- `overflow-x: hidden` on body is a safety net that could mask future regressions; nothing currently overflows.
- Zero JS console errors across six runs; resize redraw (120ms debounce) preserves scrub position.

## Questions to Consider

1. Would you dare show a stranger the confrontation before asking for their birthdate? A ghost grid costs nothing and previews the product at the moment of maximum skepticism — is the text-only hero restraint, or hiding?
2. Whose expectancy is this? Australian baseline, U.S. time-use data, global audience — does a viewer in Ohio or Mumbai experience 83.1 years as *precise*, or as someone else's number wearing precision's clothes?
3. Is the page's last word actually agency? The final interactive element is the share button, while the true agency beat — "Your week #1,733 starts Monday." — is set at 12.5px/.55 opacity, the faintest text on the page.
