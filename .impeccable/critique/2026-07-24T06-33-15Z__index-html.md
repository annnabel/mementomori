## Anti-Patterns Verdict

**Not slop — distinctive, with a real POV.** Verified in-browser at 390×844 and 1440×900 across 20+ screenshots.

**LLM assessment**: No side-stripes, gradient text, glassmorphism, cards, hero-metric tiles, numbered scaffolding, or cream default palette. Amber is verifiably reserved for "the present moment" only. Uppercase tracked labels appear at just 2–3 deliberate points, not as per-section grammar. No text overflow at any tested breakpoint. One lane-risk: the reframe fold (dark editorial serif, centered italic quote, uppercase attribution) is the one template-adjacent moment, rescued by the honesty system around it (truthful scrub labels, "where this page was made," the ember that never marks a life complete).

**Deterministic scan**: 1 CLI finding — `em-dash-overuse` (5 em-dashes in body copy; an AI-cadence tell by rule, but the em-dash is documented brand voice in DESIGN.md; borderline). In-page detector: pre-submit 1 finding (`all-caps-body`, 33 chars — this is the "WHEN WERE YOU BORN?" form label, a false positive: label, not body); post-submit 2 findings (adds `line-length` ~97 chars/line on a post-submit text block — likely the 13px sans lines in the 680px column, e.g. legend hint / small print; genuine, the LLM review missed it).

**Visual overlays**: browser evidence was collected headlessly — script injection and the in-page detector succeeded, but there is no user-visible tab, so no overlay is available to view.

## Overall Impression

This is a committed, disciplined brand surface that executes its manifesto: dark by conviction, precision as emotional engine, ending on agency. The two real problems are both at the edges of the arc: the conversion moment silently fails on desktop, and users past 80 — inside the stated audience — silently lose their place on the chart. The single biggest opportunity: make the peak-motivation moment (text someone) fail-proof everywhere.

## What's Working

1. **The honesty system is the brand, executed end-to-end** — scrub label reads "YOU ARE HERE · 33" only at your real age, degrades to "AGE 70" with an amber tick holding your true position; the ember never marks a life "complete"; assumptions are sourced and explained.
2. **Accessibility is designed, not bolted on** — canvas `aria-hidden` with caption as text equivalent and focus handoff; chart as keyboard `role="slider"` with `aria-valuetext`; DOM readout instead of canvas tooltips; reduced-motion verified working; text floor clears 4.5:1.
3. **The share card looks like the product** — same tokens, grid, ember, Newsreader stat line; the viral surface won't cheapen the brand at the hop.

## Priority Issues

- **[P1] Desktop "Text {name} now" fails silently.** `sms:` href with no capability check or feedback — the single conversion moment of the whole arc dies with a click that does nothing on most desktops. Fix: detect likely-no-handler and fall back to copying the drafted message with "Copied — send it from your phone," reusing the existing share-fallback pattern (app.js:410–414). Suggested: /impeccable harden
- **[P1] Chart hard-stops at 80 and silently drops older users.** `aria-valuemax="80"`, scrub clamp (app.js:195,204), and the you-tick drawn only when age ≤ 80 (app.js:251) mean a 68–88-year-old — inside the product's 15–75 audience — gets no "YOU ARE HERE" and a chart that ends before their present. Fix: state the data limit ("ATUS data ends at 80") and pin the scrub/label for age > 80 rather than dropping "you." Suggested: /impeccable harden
- **[P2] Chart fold is the cognitive peak right after the emotional peak.** 6 legend chips + a two-instruction hint + scrubber + 7-item readout in one fold; fails the ≤4-options and chunking checks; most "dashboard" moment on an anti-dashboard brand. Fix: default 2–3 series on (Alone, Partner, Friends — the ones the insight copy references), or cut the hint to one instruction placed after the chart. Suggested: /impeccable distill
- **[P2] The calendar has no key.** Filled vs hollow vs amber is never explained; the caption (the only decode) can sit below the fold during the 1.5s fill. Fix: one small-print line: "One dot per week. The amber dot is this week." Suggested: /impeccable clarify
- **[P3] Reveal styling makes sections 3–5 a single point of failure.** CSS ships `[data-reveal] { opacity: 0 }` (index.html:87); if the IntersectionObserver path ever fails post-submit, the bottom half of the arc is invisible — and DESIGN.md claims visible-by-default markup. Fix: default visible; apply the pre-reveal state via a JS-added class. Suggested: /impeccable harden

## Persona Red Flags

**Jordan (first-timer)**: watches dots fill for 1.5s without being told each dot is a week (no key); the legend hint asks two things before the chart is seen once. Hero is unmissable, though: one field, one button.

**Casey (distracted mobile)**: survives interruption well (numbers restated everywhere) but will bounce at the chart fold — the one screen demanding concentration. Dismissing the share sheet returns silently (AbortError) with no retry reassurance.

**Sam (accessibility-dependent)**: strongest outcome — slider semantics, focus handoff, reduced-motion all verified. Two flags: `#err` not associated to the date input via `aria-describedby`; canvas axis/label text at 10–11px is below the page's own 12.5px floor (DOM readout equivalence saves it, but scrub label legibility is marginal).

**68-year-old share recipient (project persona)**: arrives via `?age=` cleanly, but the >55 insight ("Time alone rises for the rest of life…") is the darkest note any user receives, paired with the Alone line peaking at 8h, with no reframe until Seneca. Past 80 they lose their chart position entirely (P1). Bonus-time calendar framing, by contrast, is exemplary.

## Minor Observations

- Two privacy microcopy variants; repeat one sentence verbatim — repetition reads as a promise.
- "Your week #1,733 starts Monday" — the week actually starts on the birth weekday, not Monday; brand's own bar is precision.
- The insight line doesn't respond to scrubbing; a user scrubbed to 70 may read it as describing age 70. "At your age," prefix would disambiguate.
- Locale: `en-US` date formats under Australian life-expectancy copy; use the user's locale (`undefined`).
- Post-submit `line-length` ~97 chars/line (detector): constrain the wide 13px sans lines (legend hint / small print) with a `max-width` in ch.
- Share card bottom third is dead space for shorter lifespans; the card states but doesn't invite (no hook line).
- Basis change (F/M/avg) re-renders instantly with no acknowledgment that rows were added/removed below the fold.
- Em-dash count (5) is an AI-cadence tell by detector rule; defensible as documented brand voice, but worth a pass to see which ones commas could replace.

## Questions to Consider

1. Is the chart earning its cost? Would a single pre-scrubbed frame — your age, the Alone line, one sentence — confront harder than an explorable dashboard on a page whose manifesto says "the calendar is not a metric to optimize"?
2. The recipient's hero states the friend's *lived* weeks, but the product's engine is the *remaining* count. Would "Your friend has ~2,400 weeks left. How many do you have?" restart the confrontation instead of the arithmetic?
3. The page trusts users to 99 with a birthdate but only to 80 with data. Is "bonus time" a gift, or a soft exclusion from the precision everyone else gets?
