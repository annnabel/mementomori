## Anti-Patterns Verdict

**Not AI slop — the page passes.** The execution (canvas week-grid with a single pulsing ember, real ATUS data, generated 4:5 share card, bonus-time framing) reads intentional and crafted, not templated. Honest caveats: "memento mori → candlelit dark serif + amber" is the first-guess aesthetic for this category (first-order category reflex), and Newsreader is on the reflex-reject font list — but DESIGN.md commits to both as identity, and identity-preservation wins for an existing surface.

**Deterministic scan**: CLI `detect.mjs` on index.html: **0 findings (exit 0)**. Browser-injected detector found **4**: `tiny-text` ×2 (`.legend-hint` 11.5px line 202, `.source` 11.5px line 204), `tight-leading` (`.reframe blockquote` line-height 1.18, line 222), `all-caps-body` (`.reframe .attr`, 33 chars, line 230). The attribution caps are a deliberate, single-use quote convention (acceptable); the 11.5px texts are real and compound the contrast problem below. No user-visible overlay was possible (headless environment; fallback reported).

## Cognitive Load

Low — 0–1 checklist failures. One idea per fold is executed rigorously. One decision point exceeds 4 visible options: the chart legend (6 toggle chips), acceptable as a homogeneous group. The hero stacks two decisions (birthdate + expectancy basis) before any payoff; the preselected "Average" mitigates it, but the sex control could arrive after the first render of the grid.

## Emotional Journey

Arc holds: hero (m1) is legible in <3s — headline, "See yours.", one form. Calendar (m2) lands the confrontation; the caption's exact numbers do the work. Chart section sags on mobile: the always-on tooltip box occludes the center of the chart (m3), and the age-80 axis label is clipped by the canvas overflow. Reframe (m4) is the typographic peak. Act ends on agency ("You have 2,732 weeks left. Who came to mind just now?" → prefilled SMS) — peak-end rule honored; share sits last as designed. Bonus-time path (e3) is humane, but the fully-filled grid loses the amber ember entirely — the one symbol of "still burning" disappears for exactly the users who most deserve it.

## Strengths

1. **The calendar moment works.** 52-col canvas grid, 1.5s fill, single pulsing amber current-week dot, exact caption numbers — the emotional core is executed with restraint and precision (m2).
2. **States and edges are designed, not defaulted**: gentle inline errors (e1), bonus-time framing (e3), live re-render when switching expectancy basis, reduced-motion honored for fill/pulse/reveals (r1–r3), clean keyboard tab order with visible focus (k1–k3).
3. **The token discipline is real**: amber only ever means "now"; ~97% of the surface is two neutrals; the chart palette is the one full-palette moment, as documented.

## Priority Issues

1. **[P1] The muted-ivory microcopy ramp fails its own PRD contrast floor.** Measured over #0A0A0A: `.42` → 3.56:1, `.4` → 3.35:1, `.38` → 3.10:1, `.35` (legend-off) → 2.79:1 — all below the 4.5:1 the PRD commits to. Affected: `.micro` privacy lines ("Stays on your device — we store nothing", line 117), `.small-print` (line 168), `.legend-hint` (202), `.source` (204), `.reframe .fade` (242), canvas axis labels (app.js:197, rgba .38 at 11px), legend-off chip text (199). The privacy line is explicitly part of the activation funnel. Fix: raise the ramp floor to `.55` (5.38:1) for ≤13px text, `.5` (4.63:1) minimum elsewhere; canvas axis fill to rgba(.55).
2. **[P1] Real horizontal overflow on the two most common narrow widths, masked by `overflow-x: hidden`.** Post-submit at 390px: `canvas#chart` renders 390px wide inside a 342px content box (scrollWidth 414 vs 390) because app.js:168 sizes from `.chart-sec`'s `clientWidth`, which includes its 24px side padding — the age-80 label is clipped. At 320px: the three `white-space: nowrap` `.seg` buttons total ≈336px in 272px (scrollWidth 360 vs 320). Fix: wrap the chart canvas in an unpadded div (as `#grid-wrap` already does for the grid) and allow `.seg` to wrap or shorten labels.
3. **[P1] Render-blocking third-party font chain risks the PRD's <1s first paint.** The Google Fonts CSS `<link>` (line 16) is render-blocking (browser-reported `renderBlockingStatus: "blocking"`); a slow/unreachable fonts.googleapis.com delays first paint of everything. Measured payload: 6 unique woff2 files, 516KB total; realistic English-page fetch ~278KB of the ~309KB total page weight (~90%). Under the 500KB budget, but the blocking single point of failure and 4G tail latency are the issue. Fix: self-host a subset (latin, 300/400/500 + italic 300/400) with `<link rel="preload">` + `font-display: swap`, cutting ~278KB to roughly 60–120KB and removing the third-party render dependency.
4. **[P1] The `?age=` share-recipient path silently writes a synthetic birthdate into the field** (app.js:61–67 → "01/01/1996" visible in the input, screenshot e2) directly above "Stays on your device — we store nothing." A recipient can one-tap submit the sharer's approximate data (PRD: "must confirm their own date"), and the prefilled value reads as "this site already knows my birthday" — trust dissonance at the exact moment trust is being claimed. Fix: don't prefill the input; use the age to personalize the hero copy instead (e.g. sub-line "Your friend has lived N weeks. See yours.").
5. **[P2] The share loop leaks at the link-preview step**: no `og:image` (or `og:url`), so every pasted `?age=` link renders a text-only card in iMessage/WhatsApp/Slack — the viral surface the PRD calls first-class is invisible at its widest distribution point. Also: mobile tooltip permanently occludes the chart center, and the scrub interaction is undiscoverable (hint text only mentions legend taps) and has no keyboard equivalent.

## Persona Red Flags

**Jordan (first-timer from a friend's story)**: The `?age=` prefill is the flag — a birthdate already in the field of a site claiming to store nothing (e2). Will either distrust the privacy line or submit the wrong data. "Life expectancy basis" with decimal years may read as an actuarial form moment before any payoff has been earned.

**Casey (one-handed, 4G)**: Render-blocking font CSS delays first paint on 4G precisely during the 8-second patience window. The 32px legend chips sit below comfortable thumb accuracy (six of them, 73–101px wide). Chart drag works one-handed (`touch-action: pan-y` preserved scroll ✓), but the tooltip covers what the drag reveals.

**Sam (screen reader / keyboard)**: The chart's per-age values are pointer-only — `role="img"` + label + insight line summarize, but the scrubbed data has no keyboard or text path. `aria-live="polite"` wraps the entire `#results` region, so submitting announces a wall of text (caption + small print + hint + source + insight + act prompt) in one burst. Sub-4.5:1 microcopy hits low-vision users hardest. Focus indicators and tab order are otherwise clean (k1–k3, B's tab-order trace).

## Minor Observations

- `favicon.ico` 404s on every load (console noise, missed brand moment in the tab).
- `window.scrollTo({behavior:'smooth'})` (app.js:410) ignores `prefers-reduced-motion` — the PRD says fully honored.
- Bonus-time grid has no ember at all (`current = -1`) — consider keeping the final dot pulsing.
- `#D9863B` / ivory rgba values are duplicated across CSS and JS (canvas can't read custom props directly, but a single JS constants block mirroring `:root` would prevent drift).
- `sms:?&body=` is the pragmatic cross-platform hack; verify on current iOS (occasionally drops the body).
- `.legend-hint` and `.source` at 11.5px are below comfortable minimums independent of contrast (detector `tiny-text`).
- Quote line-height 1.18 at 300 weight on dark ground is tight for two-line renders (detector `tight-leading`); 1.24–1.28 would breathe without losing the monument feel.

## Questions to Consider

- Where does the ember live for someone in bonus time? That user is the strongest testimonial the page has.
- Could the `?age=` arrival show the *sharer's* grid as a ghosted preview ("Your friend has lived 1,584 weeks") instead of impersonating the recipient's data — turning an integrity bug into a social-proof moment?
- Does the expectancy control earn hero placement, or could it be small print with an "adjust" affordance after the grid renders — halving the ask at peak skepticism?

## Audit Findings (by severity)

**P1**
- Contrast: `.42/.4/.38/.35` fg-opacity text = 3.56/3.35/3.10/2.79:1 on #0A0A0A (WCAG 1.4.3 AA fail). index.html:117,168,202,204,242,199; app.js:197. Passes start at `.5` (4.63:1). Amber 7.0:1 ✓, body ramps ≥.55 ✓, buttons 16.5:1 ✓, chart lines ≥5.5:1 vs 3:1 graphics floor ✓.
- Responsive overflow: app.js:168 (`.chart-sec` clientWidth includes padding) → chart canvas 24px overflow at 390px; `.seg` nowrap row 40px overflow at 320px (index.html:119–132).
- Render-blocking fonts.googleapis.com CSS (index.html:16); measured 6 woff2 = 516KB total, ~278KB realistic; total realistic page ≈309KB (<500KB budget ✓, but blocking + third-party ✗ for the 1s target on 4G).
- `?age=` prefill writes synthetic birthdate (app.js:61–67) — PRD share-recipient contract + trust microcopy conflict.

**P2**
- Legend touch targets 32px (<44px PRD commitment; index.html:194) — all six chips, measured 73–101×32.
- `aria-live="polite"` on all of `#results` (index.html:317) — announces entire results block at once; move to `#cap-main` only.
- No `og:image`/`og:url` — share links render without preview card.
- Chart tooltip permanently occludes chart center on mobile; scrub has no keyboard path and no text equivalent of per-age values.

**P3**
- favicon 404; smooth-scroll not reduced-motion-gated (app.js:410); bonus-time grid drops the ember; 11.5px legend-hint/source; blockquote line-height 1.18; `sms:?&body=` syntax quirk; color constants duplicated CSS↔JS.

## Positive Findings

- Zero console errors through the whole flow; clean submit → render → share path.
- 31KB of dependency-free local code; inlined dataset, no runtime fetches — the privacy claim is architecturally true.
- prefers-reduced-motion honored for fill/pulse/reveals; rAF pulse pauses off-screen via IntersectionObserver; resize debounced.
